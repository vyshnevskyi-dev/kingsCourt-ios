Hello!

Concrete League is an original companion for a fictional urban five-a-side football tournament. It contains no betting, gambling, prizes, purchases or licensed teams.

To test:
1. Browse all 32 teams and open a squad profile.
2. Open Matches, select a fixture and toggle a reminder.
3. Review the eight group tables and knockout bracket under Explore.
4. Open Courts, Stories, My League and Settings to verify their content and controls.

No account or login is required.

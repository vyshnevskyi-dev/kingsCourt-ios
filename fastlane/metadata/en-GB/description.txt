Kings Court

Your companion for live combat sports events. Whether you're watching armoured knights clash, boxers trade punches, or bareknuckle fighters go toe-to-toe, Kings Court brings the tournament to your pocket. Track every bout, follow the brackets as they unfold, and join the crowd in predicting outcomes.

What can I do with this?
Browse fighter profiles with photos, weight classes, and win-loss records. See who's climbing the rankings and which matchups are coming next. The tournament bracket updates in real time, so you'll always know who's advancing and when the next fight begins.

How do I follow the action?
Check the schedule to see which ring is active and when your favourite fighters step up. Set reminders for main-card bouts, watch the countdown to bell time, and catch judge scorecards and knockout replays after each round. Viewer polls let you pick winners and call the round—see how your instincts stack up against the rest of the arena.

Anything else?
Your ticket lives here as a QR code for quick entry. Order drinks from the bar menu and have them delivered to your seat. Browse event merchandise and grab something to remember the night.

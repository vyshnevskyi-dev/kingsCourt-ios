Kings Court

Step into the arena. Kings Court is your companion for the full tournament experience — from the moment you arrive to the final bell.

Follow every fighter on the card. Check their record, weight class, and recent form. Watch the bracket unfold in real time as winners advance and challengers emerge. See who's fighting next, set reminders, and never miss a bout.

Cast your predictions before each fight. Will it go the distance? Which round might end it? Compare your calls with the crowd and see how the room leans.

Order a drink without leaving your seat — choose from the menu, send it to your section, and stay focused on the action. Browse tournament gear and pick up a shirt or a fighter's signature item.

**What's on the card tonight?** 
The schedule shows every fight in order — undercard through to the main event. Tap any bout to see the full tale of the tape.

**How does the bracket work?** 
Each win moves a fighter up the tree. Tap any name to jump to their profile. The bracket updates live as judges announce decisions.

**Can I check in with my phone?** 
Your ticket lives here. Show the QR code at the door — VIP and general admission both scan the same way.
